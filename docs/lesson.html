Did FortiGate deny the connection?

A TCP request from a LAN client to a DMZ service fails. Use the evidence in each case to separate an explicit FortiOS policy decision from a stopped listener or a missing route.

The two fixtures are intentionally labelled synthetic. In a real lab, generate the request from the client, confirm the service from the DMZ host, then correlate the fresh Forward Traffic record by source, destination, port, time, action and policy ID.

Evidence ruleA timeout alone does not prove a firewall decision.
